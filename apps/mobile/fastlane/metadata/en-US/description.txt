Turn a quiet moment into a thoughtful daily ritual with Fortuness.

Choose an intention, draw a beautifully illustrated tarot-inspired card, and receive a grounded reflection you can carry into your day. Every reading is saved to your private account, so your card collection and personal archive can grow over time.

WITH FORTUNESS YOU CAN

- Begin with one free daily reading
- Focus on General, Love, Work, or Growth
- Reveal original artwork across a complete 78-card deck
- Revisit saved readings and discovered cards
- Filter your archive by intention, card, orientation, or date
- Sync safely across your Apple devices with Sign in with Apple
- Set an optional local reminder for your daily ritual
- Delete your account and associated data from inside the app

OPTIONAL PURCHASES

Fortuness remains usable with its included daily reading. If you want more, the 10-Reading Pack is a one-time purchase that adds credits until used. Oracle+ is an auto-renewing monthly subscription that grants up to 10 readings each day while active. Apple displays the localized price and confirms every purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Fortuness offers tarot-inspired reflections for entertainment and personal contemplation. It does not predict certain outcomes or provide medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.
